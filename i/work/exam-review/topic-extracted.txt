Primary Assessment Domain
The primary purpose of this Enterprise Capstone Project is to assess the student's ability to
design, develop, deploy, demonstrate, and defend an Enterprise Cross-Border Digital
Commerce Platform capable of managing global customers, merchants, products, inventory,
intelligent pricing, international orders, digital payments (simulated), logistics integration,
AI-assisted demand forecasting, and executive business intelligence.
Although students will use Artificial Intelligence, Cloud Computing, DevSecOps, Infrastructure
as Code, Monitoring, Cybersecurity, Software Engineering, Databases, APIs, Automation, and
Data Analytics throughout the project, these technologies are supporting implementation
technologies. The primary assessment focuses on Enterprise Digital Commerce,
Cross-Border Trade, Marketplace Engineering, Order Management, Supply Chain
Visibility, Customer Experience Management, AI-assisted Business Analytics, and
Enterprise Commerce Operations.
This project shall execute successfully on a laptop containing 8–16 GB RAM.

1. Project Background
GlobalCommerce Marketplace International operates one of the world's fastest-growing
cross-border digital marketplaces connecting buyers, sellers, logistics providers, and fulfilment
centres across multiple countries.
The organisation manages:

===== PAGE 4 =====
● Online Marketplace
● Merchant Management
● Product Catalogue
● Inventory Management
● Customer Accounts
● Order Management
● International Shipping
● Warehouse Operations
● Business Intelligence Platform
● Customer Service Operations
The organisation processes more than 5 million simulated customer transactions daily.
Current operations rely on multiple disconnected systems responsible for inventory, customer
management, payments, shipping, merchant onboarding, analytics, and reporting.
As business expands internationally, management requires a centralized enterprise platform
capable of improving operational visibility, customer experience, intelligent forecasting, and
executive decision-making.
The organisation has appointed your consulting company to design and implement a
proof-of-concept Enterprise Digital Commerce Platform.
The completed solution should demonstrate how Artificial Intelligence, enterprise software
engineering, cloud-native platforms, and intelligent analytics improve global digital commerce.
2. Business Problem
GlobalCommerce currently manages products, customers, merchants, warehouses, logistics,
inventory, pricing, and analytics using multiple independent software platforms.
This results in:
● Poor inventory visibility.
● Delayed order fulfilment.
● Overstocking.
● Stock shortages.
● Inefficient merchant onboarding.
● Limited executive reporting.

===== PAGE 5 =====
● Inconsistent customer experience.
Executive management requires an Enterprise Digital Commerce Platform capable of
integrating marketplace operations, intelligent inventory forecasting, logistics tracking, customer
management, AI-assisted demand forecasting, and executive dashboards.
The proposed solution should demonstrate how enterprise software engineering improves
operational efficiency, customer satisfaction, and business intelligence.

12. Note for Students
This Enterprise Capstone Project has been designed to assess the knowledge and practical
skills acquired throughout the Diploma in AIOPS.
Students are strongly encouraged to complete as many practical laboratories as possible from
Ar Razzaq Part 1 and Ar Razzaq Part 2 before attempting the final assessment.
Access to the Ar Razzaq laboratories requires maintaining an active membership in the
Diploma in AIOPS. Completion of these laboratories is recommended but not mandatory for
eligibility to undertake the final examination.
Students who complete the laboratories will gain substantial practical experience in Python,
FastAPI, React, PostgreSQL, Docker, APIs, monitoring, DevSecOps, Artificial Intelligence,
enterprise software engineering, cloud-native application development, and modern system
architecture. This experience is expected to significantly improve their ability to complete the
capstone project, deliver a professional presentation, demonstrate a working enterprise digital
commerce platform, and confidently defend their technical and architectural decisions during the
viva voce examination.
