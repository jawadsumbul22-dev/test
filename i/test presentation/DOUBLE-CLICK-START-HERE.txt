Double-click GlobalCommerce Exam.exe.

BEFORE EXAM DAY: click Prepare / rebuild and wait until the website opens.
ON EXAM DAY: click Start project. It starts Docker and opens your website.
Use Presentation, PDF backup and 18 diagrams buttons for your exam materials.
Use Stop project after the exam. Your saved data is preserved.

Login: admin@globalcommerce.local / Admin123!

This is a Windows launcher, not a standalone copy of the full system.
Keep Docker Desktop installed and keep the original project folder in place.
You can move this whole presentation folder to your Desktop; keep its contents
together. If you move the original project, update project-path.txt.
The launcher is locally compiled and unsigned, so Windows may show a warning.
Do not disable Windows security. Contact your administrator if execution is blocked.

No command typing is necessary. The launcher displays progress and errors.
Prepare / rebuild may need internet. Complete it before the exam, not during it.
No files are uploaded to GitHub and the launcher never resets your database.

Compilation is checked separately from live Docker startup. A complete live
startup still needs testing once Docker is available on your machine.
