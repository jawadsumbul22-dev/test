REHEARSE THE DEMONSTRATION
Use a product with sufficient stock and record the allocated warehouse.
Write down actual on-hand, reserved and available stock first.
Create a two-unit order and show the order number, quantity and charges.
Reserved should increase by two; available should decrease by two.
Progress the shipment through valid statuses to delivery.
On-hand and reserved should each decrease by two at delivery.
Show one forecast, its error and its recommendation.
Use a separate pre-dispatch order to demonstrate cancellation and released stock.
Finish with business analytics, then API monitoring.
